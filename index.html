About简介

I am a Ph.D. student in Sociology at Peking University. My research uses a Bourdieusian lens to study credentialization, symbolic capital, and elite consumption in Chinese and transnational contexts, with a long-standing interest in gender and educational inequality.

My dissertation examines China’s comprehensive quality evaluation reform in high schools (综合素质评价) and how it reshapes educational inequality, drawing on fieldwork in three high schools in Guangzhou.

Before Peking University, I earned an Ed.M. in Education Policy and Analysis from Harvard and a B.A. in Gender Studies from UCLA, and taught in rural schools in Guizhou and Gansu.

我是北京大学社会学系博士研究生。我的研究借助布迪厄的理论视角，关注中国及跨国情境中的文凭化、象征资本与精英消费，并长期关注性别与教育不平等。

我的博士论文研究高中综合素质评价改革及其与教育不平等的关系，田野工作在广州的三所高中开展。

来到北大之前，我在哈佛大学教育研究生院获得教育政策与分析硕士学位（Ed.M.），在加州大学洛杉矶分校获得性别研究学士学位，并曾在贵州和甘肃的乡村学校任教。
